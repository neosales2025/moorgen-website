What is Moorgen ?

Moorgen, is a premium smart home brand dedicated to creating intelligent, secure, and luxurious living experiences. We offer advanced smart home solutions, including digital door locks, lighting control, automation, security, and whole-home intelligence, designed with cutting-edge technology and elegant craftsmanship.

Our mission is to make everyday life smarter, safer, and more convenient through innovative products that combine reliability, modern design, and seamless automation.

Moorgen — where luxury meets intelligent living.

What is Wiikto ?

WIIKTO INDIA LLP is the authorised all-India dealer for Moorgen Digital Locks. Based in Rajkot, we deliver premium digital door locks, smart access systems, and intelligent security products designed to deliver convenience, safety, and modern living.

With a commitment to quality, advanced technology, and exceptional customer service, WIIKTO serves homeowners, businesses, builders, and channel partners across India. Our mission is to make every home and workplace smarter, more secure, and future-ready through reliable and innovative smart security solutions.
