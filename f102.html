F102 Series Moorgen Smart Lock

Achieve peace of mind with our advanced home security solution.

Dual Mode Selection

F102 Series can be customized for office use. The "Auto-Lock Disabled" mode allows for seamless entry and exit from the office without the need to wait for the door to lock and unlock every time, providing convenience in an office setting. If you need to go out, you can also press the lock button on the fingerprint lock to lock the door.

IC Card

Gain entry with a simple swipe or tap of an IC card. Enjoy the convenience of keyless access with our card unlocking method.

Convenient Life
Unlock with One Grip

Authenticating fingerprint while holding the lever — lifting the lever unlocks the door. Equipped with a doorbell function, no additional installation required.

Virtual Passcode Adds an
Extra Layer of Security

F102 Series not only supports passcode functionality but also includes a virtual passcode feature. This offers enhanced security in the presence of others, ensuring passcode confidentiality while still allowing for easy door access.

A Self-Developed App

Global compatibility, with your door lock as the focal point. Monitor indoors and outdoors in real time, unlock remotely, and stay informed about every activity at your door — anytime, anywhere.
