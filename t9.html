T9 · Smart Lock

T9 Series Moorgen Smart Lock

CNC mobile curved craftsmanship, Swarovski crystal buttons, facial recognition, fingervein recognition.

Real Swarovski Crystal
"Where German Engineering Meets Swarovski Craftsmanship"

The T9 is embellished with genuine Swarovski® crystals — a collaboration that brings the rare language of fine jewelry to the front door. Every facet is set by hand on a CNC-crafted panel, pairing German security engineering with a level of finish more often found in luxury fashion and timepieces than in a smart lock.

Visual Peephole
Real-Time Viewing

The T9 comes with a peephole and visual screen. Doorbell rings or anti-prying alarms wake the camera to take a snapshot — view it on your phone, or check who's outside in real time from the screen inside whenever you want.

4-Inch HD Display.
1 Megapixel Camera

Not only see and hear — but see and hear clearly. A large crisp display and high-definition camera mean every visitor is identified instantly.

3D Facial Recognition Technology

Requires live facial recognition. Not limited to frontal views — suitable for various angles, facial expressions, and makeup combinations. Resilient against risks like unlocking attempts using photos or videos of fake faces, providing higher security.

Children Lock

Developed by Moorgen based on adult hand data. Only when an adult presses the four fingers on the back of the door handle, covering the sensing area, and simultaneously presses the thumb on the front opening button, will the door open smoothly. This effectively eliminates the safety risk of children or pets accidentally opening the door from the inside.

Military-Grade Vein Collection
and Recognition Technology

T9 employs military-grade vein collection and recognition technology. It uses infrared penetration to illuminate the recognition area, followed by biometric camera technology for information collection. It can effectively recognize dry, wet, and peeling fingers.

A Self-Developed App

Global compatibility, with your door lock as the focal point. Monitor indoors and outdoors in real time, unlock remotely, and stay informed about every activity at your door — anytime, anywhere.

Inner and Outer Anti-Pinching Design

Engineered to address a long-standing industry challenge, keeping fingers safe on both sides of the door.

Ergonomic Handle Design

The handle panel adopts CNC technology, featuring an integrated appearance and ergonomic design.
