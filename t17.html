T17 Moorgen Smart Lock

FPC fingerprint recognition, passcode, card & app — T17 Series offers 5 ways to unlock, with one grip to open the door.

Equipped with Swedish FPC Sensor

Possesses AI autonomous learning — automatically learning and improving with each unlock, becoming more sensitive and ensuring higher stability and security over time, while effectively identifying fake fingerprints.

Unlock with One Grip

Authenticating your fingerprint while holding the lever — simply lift the lever to unlock. Equipped with a built-in doorbell function, no additional installation required.

IC Card

Gain entry with a simple swipe or tap of an IC card. Enjoy the convenience of keyless access with our card unlocking method.

Anti-Peeping Virtual Passcode

T17 Series doesn't just support passcode entry — it includes a virtual passcode feature too. Add any digits before or after your real 6-digit passcode and the door still opens, so no one watching learns your actual code.

Dual Apps Supported

No need for keys — travel light and stay convenient. T17 Series can also be integrated with the Moorgen Smart Home system via Tuya, enabling interconnected functions such as Home Mode and Away Mode, all from the Moorgen Life or Tuya app.
