T5+ · Smart Lock

T5 Series Moorgen Smart Lock

CNC crafted panel, Swarovski crystal buttons, image transmission peephole.

Real Swarovski Crystal
"Where German Engineering Meets Swarovski Craftsmanship"

The T5+ is embellished with genuine Swarovski® crystals — a collaboration that brings the rare language of fine jewelry to the front door. Every facet is set by hand on a CNC-crafted panel, pairing German security engineering with a level of finish more often found in luxury fashion and timepieces than in a smart lock.

4-Inch HD Display.
1 Megapixel Camera

Not only see and hear — but see and hear clearly. A large crisp display and high-definition camera mean every visitor is identified instantly.

Visual Peephole
Real-Time Viewing

The T5+ comes with a peephole and visual screen. Doorbell rings or anti-prying alarms wake the camera to take a snapshot — view it on your phone, or check who's outside in real time from the screen inside whenever you want.

Virtual Password

With people around, there's no need to conceal your code. Add any random digits before or after your real password — the door still opens, but no one watching learns your actual code.

Children Lock

Developed by Moorgen based on adult hand data. Only when an adult presses the four fingers on the back of the door handle, covering the sensing area, and simultaneously presses the thumb on the front opening button, will the door open smoothly. This effectively eliminates the safety risk of children or pets accidentally opening the door from the inside.

>

Multiple Alarms

T5 Series also features multiple alarms, including anti-prying alarm, low battery alarm, and anti-peeping alarm. In the event of attempted door prying, the lock will emit a piercing alarm sound and send a notification to your mobile phone. Additionally, the fingerprint reader will automatically enter a forced lock state.

A Self-Developed App

Global compatibility, with your door lock as the focal point. Monitor indoors and outdoors in real time, unlock remotely, and stay informed about every activity at your door — anytime, anywhere.

Ergonomic Handle Design

The handle panel adopts CNC technology, featuring an integrated appearance and ergonomic design.
